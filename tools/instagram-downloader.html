Asking Loader.to to prepare your options…
What is shared, exactly. This is the second exception to our local-only rule. The public Instagram link — and nothing else — is sent to Loader.to, which returns the download card. Loader.to may see your IP address and may use its own cookies. We do not control whether a post is downloadable; that depends on the account's privacy and Instagram's rules. Save only content you own or have clear permission to use, and respect copyright.

If the card does not load

  1. Confirm the post is public. Open it in a private window — if Instagram asks you to log in, the API cannot see it either.
  2. Use the direct post link (instagram.com/reel/…, /p/… or /tv/…), not a screenshot or a profile link.
  3. Press “Open in a new tab” if your browser blocks third-party frames.
  4. Wait a minute and retry: the API can be busy or rate-limited.
