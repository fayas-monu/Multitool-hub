Split PDF

Pick pages visually, then extract them into one PDF, save each page as its own file, or divide the document into equal parts. Everything happens on your device.

Page 1-2 becomes part 1, 3-5 part 2, and so on.

Tip: for a single page, use “Extract selected pages” with one tick. Your original file is never modified.
