How this works — and what leaves your device. The YouTube tool is the one exception to our “everything stays local” rule. Your public link (and nothing else — no file, no account) is sent to Loader.to, which returns the download card you see above. Loader.to may see your IP address and may set its own cookies. We do not run the download servers and cannot control their availability. Please download only videos you own or have permission to save, and follow YouTube's terms.

If the card does not load

  1. Check that the video is public and still available. Private, age-restricted and removed videos usually fail.
  2. Press “Open in a new tab”. Some browsers block cross-site frames or third-party downloads.
  3. Turn off strict tracking protection or an ad blocker for this page, then try again.
  4. The Loader.to service can be busy. Wait a minute and retry.
