Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

In short: We have no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your PDF, image and audio files are processed in your browser and are not uploaded. The only time a link leaves your device is when you use a downloader tool.

1. What we do not collect

  • We do not ask you to sign up, and we do not collect your name, email or phone number.
  • We do not upload, store or read the files you open in the PDF, image and audio tools.
  • We do not use advertising or analytics trackers on this site.

2. What happens on your device

  • File processing: files are handled in your browser’s memory and are discarded when you close or refresh the page.
  • Saved settings: your light or dark theme choice is saved in your browser’s local storage. Your chat assistant conversation is kept in session storage and disappears when you close the tab.
  • Offline cache: a service worker saves copies of the site’s pages and libraries so the tools can work offline. You can remove them in your browser’s site settings.

3. Information that reaches third parties

  • Libraries and fonts: when a page loads, your browser requests code libraries from Cloudflare (cdnjs) and fonts from Google Fonts. Those providers can see standard request data such as your IP address and browser type.
  • Downloaders: when you use the YouTube or Instagram downloader, the link you paste is sent to the Loader.to service, which shows the download options. Do not paste private links you do not want shared with that service. See Loader.to’s own policy for how it handles data.
  • Hosting: the company that hosts this site may keep normal server logs, such as IP address and time of visit.
  • Instagram: if you open the creator’s Instagram link, Instagram’s privacy policy applies.

4. Chat assistant

The Hub Assistant is a built-in helper that runs entirely in your browser. Your messages are not sent to any server and are not used to train anything.

5. Children

The Service is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

6. Your choices

  • Clear site data in your browser to remove the saved theme and offline cache.
  • Avoid the downloader tools if you do not want a link sent to Loader.to.
  • Work offline, after the first visit, to avoid any network requests.

7. Security

Because your files stay on your device, they are not exposed to our servers. No online service can promise perfect security, so keep sensitive documents on a device you trust.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Service changes. The date at the top shows the latest update.
