2. Scope of this policy

This policy applies to the MULTI TOOL HUB website and every page, tool, script, assistant and feature available through it (the “Service”). It explains what information is processed, why, where it goes, how long it is kept and what choices you have.

It does not apply to third-party websites or services you reach through links or embedded content, including YouTube, Instagram, Loader.to, cdnjs and Font Awesome. Those services have their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to read.

3. Our privacy-first design

The Service is built around a simple principle: your files should stay with you. PDF merging, splitting, editing, cropping, rotating, watermarking, numbering, compressing, converting, text extraction, image compression and audio extraction all run in your browser using JavaScript and WebAssembly-capable browser APIs. The file is read into your device’s memory and the result is generated locally.

We do not operate a file-upload endpoint for those tools. There is no account system, no cloud dashboard and no server-side file store. The Service is a set of static pages plus open-source libraries delivered by public content delivery networks.

Two features intentionally use a third party: the YouTube Downloader and the Instagram Downloader. They send a public link to Loader.to and display the returned download card. No file of yours is sent. This is explained in section 9.

4. Your files

Local tools. When you choose a file in a local tool, your browser gives the page temporary access to that file. The tool reads its bytes, processes them in memory, and prepares a download using a temporary browser object URL. We do not receive the file, its name, its contents or its metadata. The data disappears when you close or refresh the page, subject to your browser’s normal memory and download behaviour.

API tools. The YouTube and Instagram helpers do not receive or upload media files. They accept a link, validate it in your browser, and then open the Loader.to card in a new browser tab with the public link. Loader.to may then contact the relevant platform to prepare download options.

Assistant file check. If you attach a file to the Hub Assistant, the assistant reads only the file's name, size and type in your browser to suggest a tool. It does not read or upload the file's contents. Chat text is processed locally unless you explicitly connect the optional Gemini assistant described in section 11.

Downloads. Output files are created in your browser and saved by your browser to your downloads folder. We never see them. Your operating system, browser, sync service or backup software may handle them according to your own settings.

7. Technical and hosting data

The Service is served as static files by a hosting provider. Like every website, the hosting provider may automatically process standard request data such as your IP address, browser type and version, operating system, referring page, requested file, date and time, and response status. This data is used to deliver the site, keep it secure, diagnose faults and prevent abuse.

We do not combine this technical data with your identity, and we do not use it to build a profile. We do not control the hosting provider’s retention period; it is governed by that provider’s own policy and configuration. We access server logs only if necessary to investigate a security incident or a serious fault.

If you use the Service offline through the service worker, no request is made to the hosting provider for cached files, although third-party libraries may still be requested if they are not cached.

9. Loader.to disclosure

We want this to be completely clear, because it is the main exception to the local-only design:

  • When you press "Check link & show options" on the YouTube Downloader or Instagram Downloader, the public link you entered is sent to Loader.to by opening its download card in a new browser tab.
  • Loader.to returns a download card. Depending on the service and the platform, it may contact YouTube or Instagram to prepare a file.
  • No file from your device is sent to Loader.to. The API tools do not accept file uploads.
  • Loader.to may see your IP address, browser information, the link and the time of the request. It may set its own cookies or local storage on its own page. We do not receive that information.
  • We do not control Loader.to’s privacy practices, availability, security or content. Please read its privacy policy before using the downloaders.
  • If you prefer a fully local workflow, use Video to MP3 on a file you already have. It never contacts the API.

We keep the API because it is the only practical way to offer link-based downloads in a static site, and we believe you deserve a clear explanation rather than a hidden upload. If you are not comfortable with this, simply do not use those two tools; every other tool works without them.

10. Cookies and similar technologies

We do not set advertising or analytics cookies. We do not use cookies to identify you across sites. The Service uses localStorage, sessionStorage, Cache Storage and temporary in-memory object URLs for the purposes described above. These are browser storage mechanisms, not tracking cookies.

Third-party services may set their own cookies or equivalent identifiers. In particular, the Loader.to page may set cookies when you use a downloader, and the content delivery networks may use security or performance cookies. Those cookies are controlled by the relevant third party. You can block third-party cookies in your browser settings; some features, especially the downloader cards, may then stop working.

Because we do not use non-essential cookies ourselves, we do not show a cookie-consent banner. If you are in a jurisdiction that requires consent for third-party cookies, your browser’s cookie controls and the third party’s own consent mechanisms apply.

11. Voice, clipboard and assistant

Hub Assistant - local by default. The assistant normally runs as a local, rule-based helper. Your messages are matched against built-in tool descriptions and answers inside your browser. In that mode we do not send your messages to an AI provider, and this website does not operate a chat server. The conversation is saved in your browser's local storage so it survives a refresh; you can clear it through the Clear button or your browser's site-data settings.

Optional Gemini connection. The assistant also offers a separate, optional Connect Gemini action with two modes. Browser key mode lets you paste your own Google AI Studio API key. The key is held only in this tab's sessionStorage, is never written into the site's source, localStorage or a cookie, and is sent only to generativelanguage.googleapis.com in the request header. Proxy mode connects to an assistant endpoint operated by the site owner; the API key stays on that server and the endpoint receives your chat text. Nothing is sent unless you complete the connection test and then send a message. When connected, the chat messages you type (up to the most recent 16, plus the current page path) are sent to Google's Gemini API so a reply can be generated. Your PDFs, images, audio, signatures and file contents are never sent. Google processes the chat text and your key under its own terms and privacy policy, which may involve processing outside your country. Do not send confidential documents, personal data, passwords or payment details in chat, and do not enter a key on a shared or public computer.

Your control. Remote mode is never enabled automatically without stored connection details; on a new tab, the assistant starts local. The optional connection is kept only for the current tab session and is removed when you press Disconnect, press Forget key, or close the tab. Disconnecting stops further requests but does not erase messages already processed by Google; see Google's privacy policy for its retention practices. The local chat transcript can be cleared independently at any time.

Voice input. If your browser exposes the Web Speech API and you press the microphone button, the assistant uses your browser’s speech-recognition feature. Depending on your browser and operating system, audio may be processed by the browser vendor’s speech service (for example, Google, Microsoft or Apple) rather than solely on your device. We do not receive or store the audio or the transcript beyond the message that appears in your local chat history. The microphone is used only after you press the button, and your browser will normally ask for permission. You can deny or revoke microphone permission at any time.

Read-aloud. The speaker button uses the browser’s built-in speech synthesis. Depending on your platform, the voice may be generated locally or by an operating-system service. We do not receive the text or the audio. The assistant speaks only the reply you selected, truncated to a short excerpt.

Clipboard. The “Paste” button on the downloader pages reads your clipboard only after you click it. The text is placed in the link field and validated locally. Nothing is read from the clipboard in the background. The “Copy” buttons write text to your clipboard only when you click them.

File check. As explained in section 4, attaching a file to the assistant reveals only its name, size and type to the local script. The contents are not read or uploaded.

15. Retention

We do not retain your files, because we never receive them. Local preferences and chat history remain on your device until you clear them or your browser removes them. A browser API key entered for Gemini is kept only in this tab's session storage and is discarded when you press Forget key or close the tab. Hosting logs are retained for the period set by the hosting provider, typically a short operational window, unless a security investigation requires longer preservation. In proxy mode, the proxy does not write chat text to disk; Google retains and processes the request according to its own API terms and privacy policy. Loader.to and other third parties retain data according to their own policies, which we do not control.

16. Security

We take reasonable steps to keep the Service safe: static hosting reduces the attack surface, libraries are loaded over HTTPS from reputable CDNs, downloader cards open in a separate browser tab rather than an embedded frame, and the site does not accept file uploads. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

You are responsible for the security of your own device, browser profile and downloads. Use a current browser, keep your operating system updated, avoid processing sensitive files on a shared or public computer, and do not disable your browser’s security warnings. If you discover a vulnerability, please report it privately through Instagram rather than exploiting it or disclosing it publicly.

18. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

Some browsers send a “Do Not Track” signal or a Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal. Because we do not run cross-site tracking or sell personal information, there is no tracking to switch off. We honour GPC to the extent it applies to any optional third-party sharing; we do not override it. Third-party frames may have their own signal handling.

22. India & UAE jurisdiction and legal framework

Built for careful work in India & the UAE. The Service is designed for people working across both jurisdictions. Its privacy-first design — on-device file processing, explicit opt-in for AI, no advertising or analytics, and no file uploads — reflects the data-protection principles of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the UAE's Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021. The disclosures below explain how those principles apply in practice.

This section adds the disclosures required by the data-protection and technology laws of India and the United Arab Emirates. The rest of this policy still applies.

India: DPDP Act, 2023 and IT Act, 2000

  • Who decides why data is processed. The creator, FAYAS MONU, acts as the data fiduciary for any personal data processed through the Service.
  • What is processed. File contents never leave your device for the local tools. Technical request data (IP address, browser type) is seen by the host or CDN, and a link you paste into a downloader is sent to Loader.to to fetch download options. If you explicitly connect the optional Gemini assistant, the chat text you send, the current page path, standard request metadata and — in browser-key mode — the API key you entered are processed to generate a reply. Preferences are stored only in your browser (section 6).
  • Notice and consent. You choose to use a downloader and press the button; that action is your consent to sending the link. You choose to connect Gemini and press Send; that action is your consent to sending the chat text. You can stop at any time by not using the feature, by disconnecting Gemini, or by clearing the chat.
  • Your rights. You may ask to access information about personal data we process, to correct or erase it, to nominate another person to exercise your rights if you cannot, and to have a grievance redressed. See section 14 for how to ask.
  • Cross-border processing. Chat text sent through the optional Gemini connection may be processed outside India by Google. Connect Gemini only if you are comfortable with that transfer; local mode remains available and sends nothing.
  • Children. The Service is not directed at anyone under 18 and we do not knowingly process their personal data. Verifiable parental consent would be needed before we ever did.
  • Grievance Officer (IT Rules, 2021 and DPDP Act). FAYAS MONU, reachable through the contact in section 21. We acknowledge complaints within 24 hours where practicable and aim to resolve them within 15 days. If unresolved, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India once it is operational for your complaint.

United Arab Emirates: PDPL (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021)

  • Principles. Processing is lawful, fair and transparent, limited to the stated purpose, kept to the minimum, accurate where practicable, kept no longer than needed, and protected with appropriate technical and organisational measures.
  • Your rights. You may ask for information about, access to, correction or erasure of, and restriction of processing of your personal data, and you may object to processing where the law allows. We respond within a reasonable time as the law requires.
  • Cross-border transfers. Hosting providers, CDNs, Loader.to and, when you connect it, Google's Gemini API may process technical data or chat text outside the UAE. We rely on the safeguards that the law permits for such transfers, and we send no file contents abroad. You may avoid any AI-related transfer by staying in local assistant mode.
  • Complaints. Contact us first (section 21). You may also complain to the UAE Data Office or, for free-zone activity, to the DIFC or ADGM data-protection authority.
  • Cybercrime law. We may preserve and disclose technical data if a competent UAE or Indian authority lawfully requires it.
