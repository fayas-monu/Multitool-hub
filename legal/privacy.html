Privacy Policy

MULTI TOOL HUB is built so that privacy is the default, not a setting. Your files are processed in your browser, there is no account, and the small amount of information we keep is stored on your own device. This page explains exactly what that means, including the two downloader exceptions.

01Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how the MULTI TOOL HUB website and its browser-native tools handle information. It should be read with our Terms & Conditions.

“Personal data” means information that can identify you or be linked to you. MULTI TOOL HUB is designed to avoid collecting it. The service is static: there is no application server that receives your files, no database of users and no login system.

Where this policy refers to “we”, “us” or “our”, it means the operator of the MULTI TOOL HUB deployment you are using. If you are viewing a copy hosted by someone else, that host may have its own additional practices described in Section 08.

02The short version

  • Your files stay on your device. PDF, image and media tools process locally in the browser tab.
  • No account and no profile. We do not ask for a name, email address, phone number or password.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no cross-site trackers. The libraries are bundled locally, not loaded from a CDN.
  • Preferences are stored in your browser. Theme, favourites, recents and Focus Studio progress use localStorage. You can clear them at any time.
  • Two downloader exceptions. The YouTube and Instagram tools send the public link you paste to Loader.to, and the YouTube tool loads a public thumbnail from YouTube. Nothing else is shared.
  • Hosting logs may exist. The web host that delivers the page may record standard technical request data such as your IP address.

03Data we do not collect

We do not collect, request, buy, sell or rent:

  • the contents of any PDF, image, audio or video file you open;
  • filenames, page text, image pixels, audio samples or extracted tables;
  • your name, postal address, email address, telephone number or account credentials;
  • payment details (the service has no paid features and no checkout);
  • precise location, contacts, calendar, camera, microphone or health data;
  • biometric identifiers or advertising identifiers;
  • behavioural profiles, cross-site browsing history or fingerprinting signals for advertising.

Because file contents never reach us, we cannot access, inspect, restore, disclose or delete them. They remain under your control on your device.

04Files and local processing

When you choose a file, your browser grants the page temporary read access through the File API. The tool reads that file into memory, processes it with JavaScript, Canvas, PDF.js, pdf-lib or Web Audio, and offers a generated result for download. The file is not posted to an endpoint.

The same applies to ZIP packaging and document exports: the archive or document is assembled in memory and saved through a local object URL. No copy is sent to us.

Some browsers keep a download in their download history, and your operating system may index saved files. Those behaviours are controlled by your browser and device, not by MULTI TOOL HUB. If you use a shared or managed device, other people with access to that device may be able to see files you save and local preferences you set.

Refresh or close the tab to discard the in-memory working copy. Save your output first if you want to keep it. There is no server-side recovery.

07Cookies and tracking

MULTI TOOL HUB does not set cookies for advertising, analytics or authentication. It does not use tracking pixels, fingerprinting scripts or cross-site identifiers. The preference data described above uses localStorage, not cookies.

Loader.to may set cookies on its own domain when you open its download card. Those cookies are controlled by Loader.to and are not readable by MULTI TOOL HUB. Your browser's third-party cookie settings and tracking-protection features may affect that service.

We do not respond to Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signals with different behaviour because we do not track users across sites in the first place.

08Hosting and server logs

This is a static site. The computer or service that hosts the files may automatically record standard technical information when it delivers a page, stylesheet, script, font, icon or manifest. Typical entries include the requested URL, date and time, IP address, user agent, referrer, response status and bytes transferred.

Those logs are used for delivery, security, troubleshooting and abuse prevention. Their content and retention period are determined by the hosting provider, not by the application code. If you access an offline copy directly from your device, no hosting request occurs.

We do not combine hosting logs with file contents, because we never receive file contents.

09Security

Because file processing happens locally, your documents are not exposed to a conversion server, a queue or a storage bucket. There is no server-side file store to breach. The main security considerations are therefore on your own device:

  • keep your browser and operating system updated;
  • avoid processing sensitive documents on a shared or untrusted device;
  • remember that localStorage is not encrypted and can be read by someone with access to your browser profile;
  • check that the copy of the site you are using was obtained from a trusted source;
  • save important outputs promptly and keep independent backups.

No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, but we design the service to minimise the data that exists in the first place.

10Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the GDPR, the UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act or similar legislation. These can include the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, the right to data portability, and the right to complain to a supervisory authority.

Because we do not hold an account or a server-side file store, most requests can be fulfilled directly on your device:

  • Access and portability: inspect or export site data through your browser's developer tools or settings.
  • Deletion: clear site data for this origin in your browser. This removes preferences, recents and Focus Studio progress.
  • Objection or restriction: simply stop using the relevant tool and avoid the downloader services.
  • Non-discrimination: we do not sell personal information and do not offer a paid tier, so exercising a privacy right cannot reduce your access.

If you believe the hosting provider holds request logs about you, contact that provider or the operator of this deployment. We cannot delete logs we do not control, but we will help identify the appropriate route where possible.

11Children

MULTI TOOL HUB is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Because there is no account and no server-side profile, the service does not create a child-specific data record. Parents and guardians should supervise any use by younger children, especially around downloaded third-party content.

12Retention

Preferences and local records remain in your browser until you clear them, they expire under browser policy, or the browser removes them to free space. They are not backed up by us. Files you download remain wherever you save them and are governed by your own retention practices.

Hosting logs, if any, are retained according to the hosting provider's policy. We do not operate a separate analytics archive.

13Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the service, the law or our practices change. The effective date at the top of the page will be revised. Material changes will be highlighted on the site where practicable. Because the build is static, older offline copies may continue to show an earlier version; the hosted page is the current one.

14Contact

Questions, corrections or privacy requests can be raised through the contact details published alongside the deployment of this build. Please identify the page and the feature involved. Do not send us your documents: we cannot receive them, and the service is designed so that you never need to.

Privacy by architecture. The strongest protection here is that your files never leave your device. If a future version ever adds a server-side feature, this policy and the relevant tool interface must be updated before that feature is used.
